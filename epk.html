Siara Taylor

Canadian singer-songwriter Siara Taylor crafts heartfelt indie rock with singer-songwriter roots, finding beauty and connection in life’s most vulnerable moments. Her emotionally honest lyricism and dynamic arrangements draw inspiration from artists like Maggie Rogers, Phoebe Bridgers, and Noah Kahan.

After beginning her live career through local open mics and busking across the Lower Mainland in 2022, Siara taught herself music production and built her bedroom studio, where she independently wrote, recorded, and produced her EPs In Shades of Blue (2023) and Square One (2026).

With more than 100,000 views across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Ultimate Guitar, Siara has built a growing audience, earning opportunities to perform with Uptown Live Summer Series, Vancouver International Airport, and TransLink. Known for her intimate songwriting and captivating live performances, Siara Taylor continues to establish herself as an emerging voice in Canada's indie music scene. Her latest EP, Square One, featuring the single “The Parts That Remain”, is available on all streaming platforms.
